About EQS

#1 in tropical music.

A Latin music label representing artists nationally and internationally — record label, production, management, and bookings under one roof.

EQS Música was founded in Boston in 1998 by audio engineer Derek Deler — DerekVinci — and moved to Orlando, Florida in 2006. What started as a studio became a label: EQS now records, produces, manages, and books a roster of artists across bachata, merengue, salsa, kizomba, Latin pop, and urban Latin.

The sound is cinematic and the reach is global — EQS releases play across the Americas, Europe, and Asia, and the roster tours wherever tropical music is danced.
