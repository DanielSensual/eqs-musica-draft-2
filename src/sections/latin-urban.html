Led by

DSTAR LC (Dayron)

Songwriter · Producer · Arranger · Havana, Cuba / Dominican Republic

Born on November 13, 1993, in Boyeros, Havana, Cuba, DSTAR LC is a Cuban songwriter, music producer and arranger who built his career on perseverance, self-teaching and a deep passion for music. In 2008, in secondary school, he realized music was his calling and took his first steps as a songwriter and performer with the youth group Los Baby 2002.

He wanted to study music formally, but without that opportunity he taught himself: no teachers, no academy, just production software, sounds and recording techniques. Discipline and curiosity turned into real skill in songwriting, production, arranging and vocal work, and he began performing in bars and local stages and working in recording studios.

To move to the Dominican Republic, he turned his songwriting into his livelihood, writing and selling about ten songs a week. His music paid his way out of Cuba, and the Dominican Republic became his second home, where he has kept building his career alongside emerging and established artists from Europe and the Americas.

Over his career DSTAR LC has worked on projects and songs connected to major names in Latin music, including Wisin, Ñengo Flow, Descemer Bueno, Yotuel, Jacob Forever, Chacal, Diván, DJ Unic, DJ Conds and La Nueva Especie, as well as vocal arranging and production with DJ Unic on projects for artists like J Álvarez. 2025 marked a major expansion, working directly with Jacob Forever, Chacal, Diván and Descemer Bueno. His work has millions of streams across digital platforms.

Today he is focused on developing new Cuban talent in urban music and reparto, working hand in hand with DerekVinci and EQS Música to connect reparto cubano with new audiences and international markets. He also works closely with Cuban producer La Nueva Especie on new productions, bringing his experience in songwriting, production, arranging and musical direction to a new generation of artists.

For DSTAR LC, music has never been just a profession: it is what let him build his future, cross borders and turn his dreams into reality.
